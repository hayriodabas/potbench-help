Privacy

Potbench keeps everything on your device. It has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

What stays on the device

Your repots, pots, mixes, materials, bag prices, journal notes and photos are stored in the app's own folder on your iPhone or iPad. The app never sends them anywhere. iOS may include that folder in your device or iCloud backup according to your own backup settings.

Photos

A photo you add to the journal is copied into the app's folder through the system photo picker, which runs outside the app; Potbench never reads your photo library. The copy is deleted when you remove the entry or erase everything in Settings.

Reminders

A check-back reminder is a local notification scheduled on the device. iOS asks for notification permission the first time you choose one; nothing about it leaves the device.

Purchases

Potbench Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; the app only learns whether the purchase is active. Apple's privacy policy applies to the transaction.

Backups and sharing

A backup is a file you export yourself. Shared plans, batches and CSV files go through the system share sheet to the destination you choose, and that destination handles the copy under its own practices.

Network

Potbench opens no network connection of its own. The only connections are the App Store's, for the purchase, and the links on this page.
