Privacy

This prototype stores repotting projects, measurements, mix recipes and observations in the app's local storage on this device. It does not send that content to a developer server.

Projects stay on this device

Project data and estimates are processed on-device. The app has no account, advertising, analytics or third-party tracking SDK. iOS may include app data in device backups according to your device backup settings.

Exports are your choice

When you choose to create or share a shopping list or backup, iOS presents its standard document or share interface. The recipient app or destination handles the exported copy under its own practices. The app does not upload it to a developer service.

Connection status

The app checks whether a network path is available so it can display connectivity status. It does not use that check to transmit your project content.

Purchases

No production in-app purchase product is configured in this prototype build.
